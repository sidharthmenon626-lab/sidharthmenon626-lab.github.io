# Sidharth Menon — Business & Data Analyst Portfolio

> An interactive, executive portfolio website showcasing 6 production-grade data systems across **Revenue Retention**, **Product Growth & Experimentation**, **SQL Business Intelligence**, and **Data Warehousing**.

---

## ⚡ Featured Case Studies

1. **[Enterprise Analytics Data Platform](https://github.com/sidharthmenon626-lab/end-to-end-data-pipeline)** — Kimball Star Schema, Airflow 3, dbt-core (103 tests), SCD Type II, 1.15M+ records.
2. **[B2B SaaS Churn Prediction & Retention Engine](https://github.com/sidharthmenon626-lab/customer-churn-prediction-sql)** — Leakage-free ML classifier preserving $118k+ in ARR, threshold optimization (p=0.35/0.20).
3. **[E-Commerce Demand Forecasting ML](https://github.com/sidharthmenon626-lab/demand-forecasting-ml)** — Rolling-origin cross-validation, Huber Regressor (19.20% WMAPE, +22.3% error reduction over naive).
4. **[PulseFlow: A/B Testing & Product Analytics Audit](https://github.com/sidharthmenon626-lab/ab-testing-product-analytics)** — Statistical audit of 50k-user homepage test, mobile friction collapse (-31pp drop), CPO decision memo.
5. **[E-Commerce Founder Analytics: 10 SQL Queries](https://github.com/sidharthmenon626-lab/sql-business-insights)** — Diagnosed 80% revenue volume slide, whale spend concentration (top 40% spenders = 88% rev), UPI payment recovery.
6. **[B2C vs B2B Growth & Unit Economics](https://github.com/sidharthmenon626-lab/sql-product-analytics)** — Dual-warehouse comparative analytics (2-day B2C activation vs 417-day B2B expansion).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 18, TypeScript, Vite
